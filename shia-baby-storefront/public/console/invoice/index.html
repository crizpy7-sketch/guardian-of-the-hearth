Operator sign-in

Enter your console token (SHIA_CONSOLE_TOKEN). Kept in this tab only.

1. Paste the invoice

One line per item. Tabs, commas or pipes all work — paste straight from a spreadsheet or a supplier PDF. Expected order: name, size, cost, qty. Anything unrecognised becomes a blank row you can fix by hand.

2. Price it

Retail is cost × markup, rounded to your chosen ending. Change any price by hand afterwards.

3. Check the rows

SKUs must be unique. Blank ones are generated from the name and size.

Paste an invoice to begin.

4. Send to Square

Creates or updates these items in your Square catalog and sets stock to the counted quantity. Your cost is never uploaded — only retail, SKU, UPC and quantity. Items arrive not listed online; list them from the console when you're ready to sell them.
